CHAPTER 01 / THE HUMAN

Meet Maya.

Maya opens an online clothing store. She browses. She compares. She clicks.

Her name and perspective are part of our storytelling. Her click sequence comes from a real anonymous session, 18817, in the historical dataset.

We cannot know her thoughts, all the items she saw, or whether she purchased anything.

CHAPTER 05 / WHAT WE CAN SAY

Data can show what happened.
Not always why.

01

We can observe

Recorded click counts, product categories, page positions, and the order of clicks within a session.

02

We cannot infer

Product exposure, intent, attention, conversion, or a causal effect of position on clicking.

03

What to test next

Track product impressions and run carefully controlled interface experiments to understand discoverability.
